## Tampa Bay Rays @ Cleveland Guardians, 10:00a

### Summary

Game 3 of the ALCS, series tied 1-1, and both the storylines and the stat lines make this one worth clearing your schedule for. **This is the best-scored game on the board today, and it comes with actual postseason stakes.** With a gNERD of 15.99, it sits near the top of today's slate and well above the historical average, driven mostly by a genuinely excellent pitching matchup.

The series itself has been a seesaw: the series is tied 1-1 after Cleveland won 7-3 in Game 1 and Tampa Bay came back with a 3-1 win Tuesday, and weather forced an afternoon start. On the mound, Gavin Williams is the headliner — his 12.55 pNERD is elite, backed by a 97.4 mph average fastball, a 69 xFIP-, and a swinging-strike rate that would make most hitters cry uncle. He's been even better than his regular-season numbers suggest lately: Williams has been excellent this postseason, going 1-0 with a 1.54 ERA, 24 strikeouts and a 0.94 WHIP in 11⅔ innings, including 5⅔ scoreless innings against Chicago with twelve strikeouts. The only cloud is workload — Cleveland has leaned on him quite a bit lately, with two postseason starts and a relief appearance in between, so watch how deep he's allowed to go. Griffin Jax, opposing him, isn't chopped liver either (7.71 pNERD, 95.6 mph heat, strong xFIP-), though his playoff sample is tiny. Team-wise, Cleveland's tNERD edge comes from youth and a strong bullpen, while Tampa's barrel rate woes drag both sides down — but with stakes like these, that barely matters.
