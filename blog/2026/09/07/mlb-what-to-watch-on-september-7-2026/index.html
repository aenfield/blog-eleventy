Summary

The headline is Chase Burns, whose 10.38 pNERD is well above the 95th percentile historically — he's been outstanding in 2026 with a 1.16 WHIP and a 28.9% strikeout rate that ranks among the best in the National League, fueled by upper-90s velocity and elite whiff rates.
