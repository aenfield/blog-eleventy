Milwaukee Brewers @ New York Mets, 4:10p

Summary

Jacob Misiorowski is essentially a top-of-the-line watchability lock, and this Brewers-Mets series finale delivers plenty else besides. Misiorowski, in his sophomore season, has been electric, leading MLB in ERA (1.68), strikeouts, and WHIP, and his 100+ mph heat produces the kind of swing-and-miss stuff (elite SwStr%, absurd xFIP-) that fuels his 15.57 pNERD, one of the best you'll see all year. He's opposed by Sean Manaea, a serviceable but unspectacular lefty (4.31 ERA, 4-5) who's been homer-prone this year; his 5.60 pNERD is fine but nowhere near Misiorowski's level. The Brewers own the majors' best record and bring a well-rounded 9.92 tNERD built on baserunning, bullpen depth, and a young roster, while the Mets counter with a middling 5.47 mark, held down by shaky batting and baserunning but boosted by broadcast appeal. Milwaukee already took the series opener 8-1 behind Brice Turang and Sal Frelick, and the finale caps a series that's had playoff-atmosphere intensity despite the Mets' fade from contention. With an 18.28 gNERD score, this ranks near the very top of today's slate and comfortably above the historical average, making it the easy pick if you can only watch one game.
