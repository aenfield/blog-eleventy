Los Angeles Dodgers @ Atlanta Braves, 4:15p

Summary

A genuine ace-on-ace pitching matchup with playoff stakes attached — this one's worth clearing your schedule for. Chris Sale and Yoshinobu Yamamoto rank second and third in the NL in ERA, respectively, and both come in riding excellent underlying numbers to match, which is exactly the kind of era-defying, throwback pitching duel this sport doesn't offer often enough.

In an August series that has felt more like October baseball, a pair of star pitchers with playoff experience will highlight a game with postseason implications. The Dodgers enter riding a six-game winning streak but now try to avoid their first sweep by the Braves since May 2013, after the Braves won the first two games in dramatic fashion — 4-3 on Tuesday before Ha-Seong Kim delivered a walk-off RBI single Wednesday, his first hit since June 3. Sale's stuff is elite (10.05 pNERD, boosted by a sparkling xFIP-) even if the Braves' offense has left him hanging — he's pitched three straight quality starts in August only to lose each one as Atlanta's bats have produced just five runs across three games. Yamamoto isn't far behind at 7.97 pNERD, and he's 1-0 with 10 scoreless innings in two career outings against Atlanta, including a one-hitter in a 2-1 win last year. With gNERD at 15.94 — comfortably above both the historical 95th percentile (15.60) and today's slate average (11.70) — and the Dodgers' top-tier lineup (8.26 tNERD) lurking behind Yamamoto, this has both craftsmanship and drama.
