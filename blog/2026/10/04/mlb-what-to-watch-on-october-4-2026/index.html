Atlanta Braves @ Los Angeles Dodgers, 5:00p

Summary

This is Game 2 of the NLDS, and the Dodgers have a near-unhittable Blake Snell on the mound while Atlanta still hasn't even named a starter — that imbalance alone tells you plenty about where this series is heading. Los Angeles took Game 1 behind Tarik Skubal's three-homer outburst, and now hands the ball to Snell, who went 4-1 with a 1.90 ERA over 42.2 innings down the stretch; his 9.21 pNERD (well above today's 7.14 average and comfortably into the 95th percentile historically) is built on elite whiff and chase numbers and a sub-75 xFIP-, not just shiny run prevention. Atlanta's mystery starter carries a 0.00 pNERD simply because we have no data to grade him, which, with Chris Sale already used in relief and several Braves starters banged up, isn't a great sign for competitive balance. On the team side, the Dodgers' 8.84 tNERD dwarfs Atlanta's 5.17, fueled by elite batting runs, barrel rate, and a dominant bullpen — though note L.A.'s offense has actually been a bit lucky, so some regression wouldn't shock. At 14.12, the gNERD sits a tick below today's slate average but still well above the historical norm — watchable mostly because of Snell and the stakes, less so because of competitive doubt.
