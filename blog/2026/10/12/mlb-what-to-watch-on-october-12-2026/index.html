ALCS Game 1 arrives with the Rays favored to roll, but the Guardians have already proven they don't care much for predictions. The Rays dominated the regular-season series against the Guardians, finishing 5-1 in 2026, and oddsmakers clearly remember that, installing Tampa Bay as a heavy home favorite.
