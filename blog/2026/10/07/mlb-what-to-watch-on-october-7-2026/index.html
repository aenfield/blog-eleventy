Tyler Glasnow draws the ball for Los Angeles carrying the highest pNERD on the board, and his 5-2 record with a 3.53 ERA in 13 regular-season games undersells a filthy strikeout arm backed by elite xFIP-; he's also no stranger to October, though his career 2-6 record with a 4.43 ERA in the postseason suggests the Braves lineup can at least dream. Tampa Bay, fresh off clinching its fifth division title and a 2-0 series lead via 1-0 and 5-2 wins, sends out Nick Martinez, who went 15-6 with a 3.10 ERA, a 1.09 WHIP and 109 strikeouts during the regular season — solid bulk innings, though his modest pNERD (3.31) reflects middling whiff rates and a strike-heavy approach without much shine. Across the mound, Max Fried has been the Yankees' best hope, having come into this game after a strong first start of the playoffs, holding the Red Sox to just a solo homer in six innings; his 4.47 pNERD is driven by a strong xFIP-, offset by a plodding pace that drags the component down.

On the team side, the Yankees' 7.11 tNERD edges Tampa Bay's 4.51, powered by a hard-hit, barrel-happy lineup, though New York's -33 run luck suggests this offense has actually underperformed its skill level and could be due for a correction. The Rays counter with elite barrel suppression but a shaky bullpen number. At a combined 9.70, the gNERD sits below the historical average and well under today's slate — this isn't a statistical showcase, but with New York's season on the brink, the stakes alone make it worth your attention.
